1. ENTRY POINT & CORE APPLICATION SHELL
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 index.html, main.jsx & App.jsx
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- index.html:
  Sets the aerospace cyber-dark background `#0A0F1F`, configures viewport meta tags, preconnects to
  fonts, and mounts the React root `<div id="root"></div>`.
- src/main.jsx:
  Initializes React 19 root using `createRoot(document.getElementById('root'))` and wraps the app
  in `<React.StrictMode>`.
- src/App.jsx:
  Configures route hierarchy with React Router v7. Wraps the app in:
  - `<ThemeProvider>`
  - `<AuthProvider>`
  - `<SatelliteProvider>`
  Implements route-level lazy loading (`React.lazy()`) for all primary views:
  `/dashboard`, `/satellites`, `/satellites/:id`, `/orbit`, `/studio`, `/simulator`, `/missions`,
  `/communications`, `/ground-stations`, `/telemetry`, `/analytics`, `/reports`, `/alerts`,
  `/notifications`, `/admin`, `/admin/users`, `/admin/logs`, `/settings`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 Styling Architecture (index.css & App.css)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Defines aerospace color tokens:
  - Deep Space Canvas: `#050810`, `#0A0F1F`, `#070C18`
  - Luminous Telemetry Cyan: `#00F0FF`
  - Status Neon Green: `#00FF66`
  - Caution Amber: `#FFB000`
  - Critical Alarm Red: `#FF2A2A`
  - Avionics Violet: `#C084FC`
- Glassmorphism Utilities:
  Custom CSS classes for semi-transparent backdrops (`backdrop-blur-md`, subtle border glows,
  radar sweep animations, scanline overlays).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 State Contexts (src/context/)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `AuthContext.jsx`:
  Stores the authenticated user profile, token, and role. Manages `login(credentials)`,
  `logout()`, and token persistence in `localStorage`.
- `SatelliteContext.jsx`:
  Maintains active satellite selection across pages. When an operator selects a CubeSat in the fleet,
  the selection propagates to the 3D Orbit Viewer, Studio, and Alert Drawer.
- `ThemeContext.jsx`:
  Toggles between deep space dark mode and high-contrast tactical night vision.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 apiClient.js (Central Axios Engine)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Creates the Axios instance with `baseURL: import.meta.env.VITE_API_URL || 'http://localhost:4000/api'`.
- Request Interceptor: Automatically inspects `localStorage` for `orbitalx_token` and attaches

4. 3D ORBITAL VIEWER & SHADER ENGINE (src/components/3d/)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Earth.jsx, Scene.jsx & earthShaders.js
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `Earth.jsx`:
  Constructs a procedural WebGL globe using Three.js sphere geometry.
  Applies multi-texture shaders: day diffuse map, night city lights emissive map, bump map,
  and atmospheric glow haze.
- `earthShaders.js`:
  Custom GLSL vertex and fragment shaders. Calculates sunlight direction vector and blends day
  diffuse texture with night city lights along the terminator line using smoothstep functions:
    float sunIntensity = dot(vNormal, uSunDirection);
    vec4 dayColor = texture2D(uDayTexture, vUv);
    vec4 nightColor = texture2D(uNightTexture, vUv);
    gl_FragColor = mix(nightColor, dayColor, smoothstep(-0.1, 0.1, sunIntensity));
- `Scene.jsx`:
  Renders the Three.js Canvas with OrbitControls, starfield background particles, ambient lighting,
  and directional solar lighting.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 OrbitalViewer.jsx
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Plots orbits for all active constellation satellites.
- Computes Keplerian elliptical curves from semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, and RAAN.
- Renders global ground tracking stations as pulsed cyan antennas on the globe.
- Draws parabolic Bezier communication arcs between satellites and ground stations when within line of sight.

====================================================================================================
5. 3D CUBESAT DESIGN STUDIO & SIMULATION ENGINE (src/pages/studio/)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 CubeSatStudio.jsx & CubeSatCanvas.jsx (CAD Assembly Canvas)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Supports standard **1U, 2U, and 3U** CubeSat chassis configurations.
- Assembly Modes:
  - `ASSEMBLE`: Exploded view animation where modules slide smoothly outward along their mounting rails.
  - `VALIDATE`: Sweeps a luminous cyan laser line vertically through the CubeSat chassis, inspecting
    connector alignments and mass/power budget constraints.
  - `NORMAL`: Compact operational flight configuration.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 cubesatComponents.js & Component Library
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defines physical specifications for components:
- Avionics: PC104 On-Board Computers (Mass: 90g, Power: 0.8W, Vol: 0.2U)
- Power: Triple-junction GaAs Solar Panels (Power Gen: +7.2W) & Li-Ion Battery Pack Block (120g, Vol: 0.3U)
- Comms: Deployable Spring Whip Antenna & S-Band Transmitters (1.2W)
- ADCS: 3-Axis Reaction Wheels (Brass Flywheels) & Magnetorquer Rods
- Payload: Nadir Optical Imager Camera & Multispectral Radiation Spectrometer

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Procedural Mesh Generators (src/pages/studio/utils/)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `buildChassisFrame.js`:
  Procedurally constructs standard 10x10x10cm (1U), 10x10x20cm (2U), or 10x10x30cm (3U) structural
  extrusion rails using Three.js BoxGeometry and anodized black aluminum metallic materials.
- `buildComponentMesh.js`:
  Constructs detailed 3D meshes for components: green PCB substrates, gold-plated headers, copper coils,
  brass flywheel discs, and optical lenses.
- `buildSimSolarWings.js`:
  Generates deployable accordion solar array wings with dark blue photovoltaic cell textures.
- `buildSimPropulsionAndThermal.js`:
  Generates cold-gas micro-thruster nozzles, heat straps, and radiator plates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Real-Time Physics Budgeting Engine
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Calculates physical flight margins dynamically as parts are added:
- Total Mass (g) vs. Launch Adapter Payload Limit (max 4000g for 3U)
- Total Volume (U) vs. Chassis Envelope
- Power Generation (+W) vs. Operational Load (-W)
- Eclipse Battery Runtime (minutes in Earth shadow before minimum voltage cutoff)
- Bill of Materials (BOM) total cost ($) with instant CSV export.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5 CubeSatSimulator.jsx & CubeSatSimulationCanvas.jsx
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-time physics simulator placing the assembled CubeSat in orbit.
- Simulates solar exposure based on attitude, reaction wheel desaturation maneuvers, and telemetry streams.
- SimCommandConsole allows operators to dispatch telecommands directly into the simulated flight computer.
